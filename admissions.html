General Admission Requirements

River Valley College welcomes first-time, transfer, and returning students. Typical first-year applicants should provide:

  • A completed admission application
  • An official high-school transcript or equivalent
  • Any available ACT or SAT scores for placement purposes
  • Previous college transcripts, if applicable

Tuition & Costs

River Valley College works to keep tuition competitive for students and families. Costs vary by program and course load, so students receive an individualized estimate before registration.

Financial Aid

Students may qualify for grants, scholarships, work-study, or student loans. The Financial Aid Office can help students understand each option before making a decision.
