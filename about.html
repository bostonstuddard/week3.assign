About River Valley

A small college with a strong community

We help students turn curiosity and hard work into real opportunities.

Our Story

River Valley College was founded in 1968 to give students in the surrounding region greater access to higher education. What began as a small local college has grown into a modern campus serving students from many different backgrounds.

Although the college has grown, its focus remains the same: provide high-quality education in a setting where students are known, supported, and encouraged to succeed.

Our Mission

River Valley College provides accessible, practical, and engaging education that prepares students to contribute to their careers and communities.

Our Community

The campus is designed to feel welcoming and easy to navigate, with classrooms, study areas, green spaces, student services, and activities located close together.
